Common Questions

Before you sign up

How is SimpleAudit different from ConnectSecure or Network Detective?

ConnectSecure and Network Detective are excellent tools — they generate Executive Summaries and Technical Reports, and they've been around longer than us. The core difference: SimpleAudit is the only tool that automatically generates a Business Proposal PDF from your scan data — translating technical findings into a client-ready sales document. We're also significantly cheaper at entry level ($49/mo vs. $300+/mo) and require no long-term contract.

Do I need IT credentials or domain admin access to run a scan?

No credentials are required for the Standard scan. It runs from any Windows machine on the client's network and assesses the security posture from that machine's perspective. Our Pro build (available in Enterprise plan) adds optional credentialed WMI and Active Directory scanning for deeper findings — but that's not required to get a complete, useful assessment.

What does "up to X client companies" mean?

Each "company" is a separate client in your portal — with their own scan history, reports, and magic-link portal. Within each company, you can run unlimited scans on any number of machines. The limit is on how many distinct clients you manage at once, not on scan volume. You can archive old clients and add new ones as your roster changes.

Can I white-label this completely — is SimpleAudit visible to my clients?

SimpleAudit is completely invisible to your clients on every plan. Every PDF, every client portal page, and every automated email shows your MSP's name, logo, phone, and website. Your clients will never know what tool you're using.

Is the scan agent going to trigger antivirus on the client machine?

The Standard agent is built in pure Python with no offensive security binaries, so it runs cleanly through Windows Defender and most business antivirus solutions. If a specific AV flags it, right-clicking → "Unblock" or adding a folder exclusion resolves it in under a minute. We always recommend briefing the client before running any scan — both as good practice and so they're not surprised by the process.

What is the Business Proposal PDF and how is it generated?

After a scan completes, you can click "Generate Business Proposal" in your portal. SimpleAudit takes the scan findings, maps them to risk categories and estimated financial exposure, adds your MSP's recommended services and contact information, and generates a professionally formatted PDF proposal — automatically. No template editing, no copy-pasting. Clients receive a document that reads like it took hours to produce. It typically takes under a minute to generate.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, anytime. Upgrading takes effect immediately. Downgrading takes effect at the next billing cycle. If you downgrade to a plan with fewer company slots than you currently have active, you'll be asked to archive clients down to the new limit before the change processes.

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to all features on that tier. No credit card required to start. At the end of the trial, you'll choose to subscribe or your account enters a read-only state — your data is never deleted.
