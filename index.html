Common Questions

Before you sign up

How is SimpleAudit different from ConnectSecure or Network Detective?

SimpleAudit combines endpoint scanning, network scanning, M365 security checks, breach monitoring, and threat intelligence into a single scan — most competing tools require separate products or paid add-ons for each. We also generate a Business Proposal PDF automatically from your scan data, which ConnectSecure and Network Detective don't. Plans start at $49/mo with a 14-day free trial, no contract, and no setup fee.

Do I need IT credentials or domain admin access to run a scan?

No credentials are required for the standard scan. The agent runs from any Windows machine on the client's network and assesses the security posture from that machine's perspective. Optional M365 checks require read-only Azure AD credentials, which you enter per-scan — they're never stored on the client machine.

What does "up to X client companies" mean?

Each "company" is a separate client in your portal — with their own scan history, reports, and client portal link. Within each company, you can run unlimited scans on any number of machines. The limit is on how many distinct clients you manage at once, not on scan volume. You can archive old clients and add new ones as your roster changes.

Is the scan agent going to trigger antivirus on the client machine?

The SimpleAudit agent is a lightweight compiled binary with no offensive security tooling — no exploit frameworks, no password crackers, no network attack tools. It runs cleanly through Windows Defender and most business antivirus solutions. If a specific AV flags it, adding a folder exclusion resolves it in under a minute. We always recommend briefing the client before running any scan.

What is the Business Proposal PDF?

After a scan completes, you can generate a Business Proposal from your portal. It takes the scan findings, maps them to risk categories, adds your recommended services and contact information, and generates a branded PDF — automatically. No template editing, no copy-pasting. It typically takes under a minute to generate.

How does this save me time compared to what I'm doing now?

A manual assessment typically takes 2–4 hours per client: running individual tools, consolidating output into a spreadsheet, writing the report, and formatting a proposal. SimpleAudit does the scanning, enrichment, and report generation in under 20 minutes — and produces three branded PDFs automatically. For most MSPs, the time savings on a single assessment covers the monthly subscription cost.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, anytime. Upgrading takes effect immediately. Downgrading takes effect at the next billing cycle. If you downgrade to a plan with fewer company slots than you currently have active, you'll be asked to archive clients down to the new limit.

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to all features on that tier. No credit card required to start. At the end of the trial, you choose to subscribe or your account enters read-only mode — your data is never deleted.

What operating systems does the scan agent support?

The agent runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11. It's designed for client endpoint environments, which are overwhelmingly Windows in the MSP market. The portal and all report generation work from any modern browser on any OS.
